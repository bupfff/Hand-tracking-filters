Portal filters

Hold up both hands with your index fingers and thumbs out. A portal opens between them.

  • Pinch to switch. Bring your hands together, or pinch each index to its thumb.
  • Swipe to go back. Flick the portal right for the previous filter, left for the next.
  • Tilt to tune. Raise one hand above the other to dial the filter up or down.
  • Fist to freeze. Make a fist to pin the portal in place. Make a fist again to release it.
